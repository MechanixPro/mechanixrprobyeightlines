Questions, answered.

Do you service bikes at home?

Yes. A certified Mechanix Pro mechanic comes to your home, office or roadside in our service areas. If a job needs workshop tools, we pick up the bike only after you share a one-time code, and bring it back the same way.

How do I confirm my booking?

Send your build on WhatsApp. We confirm a slot and send a secure Razorpay link to pay ₹199. That amount is adjusted in your final bill.

What if more work is needed?

The mechanic shows you what is wrong and sends an itemised estimate. Nothing extra is done or charged unless you approve it.

Which bikes do you service?

All major brands: Honda, Hero, TVS, Bajaj, Royal Enfield, Yamaha, Suzuki, KTM and electric scooters for tyres, brakes and general checks.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel free up to 2 hours before your slot and the ₹199 is refunded. See our refund policy.

Is there a warranty?

Labour is covered for 15 days. Parts carry the manufacturer's warranty.
