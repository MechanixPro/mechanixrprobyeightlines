What you get.

  • Petrol bikes and scooters

    Engine oil level and filters in Basic and Standard, an engine oil change in Full, plus spark plug, chain, brakes, clutch and a safety check, in Basic, Standard and Full packages.

  • Electric scooters and bikes

    Range, charging, brakes, tyres, suspension, lights, wiring and display checks. No engine oil to change, so no engine-oil charges on EV pages.

  • Problem check for either

    Something feels wrong? A problem check finds the cause and quotes the fix before work starts.

  • Roadside help for both

    Puncture, a bike that will not start, or a scooter that stopped: share your location and we send the nearest mechanic.

  • Honest about what we can fix

    If an EV fault needs the maker's service centre, we say so plainly instead of guessing.

  • Digital service records

    Each visit is saved against your bike, petrol or electric, so its history is always on hand.

How it works.

  1. 1Tell us if it is petrol or electric, the model and your area.
  2. 2We send the right package or a problem check, with the price.
  3. 3A mechanic visits at your slot and shows you the findings.
  4. 4You approve any extra work first, then get your invoice.

Questions we get asked.

Do you service electric scooters at home?

We do doorstep checks for electric scooters and bikes: range drop, charging problems, brakes, tyres, suspension, lights, wiring and display issues. Some faults need the maker's service centre, and we tell you if so.

What does an EV check cost?

Electric bikes use the problem check, from ₹799, GST included.

Is petrol bike service different?

Yes. Petrol bikes get Basic, Standard or Full service with filters, spark plug, chain and brakes, and an engine oil change in Full service (and in Standard for 251 to 500cc). See the Services page for what each one covers.
