Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026. This policy follows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Who we are

Mechanix Pro is operated by NOVA VENTURES, Haralur Main Rd, 1st Sector, HSR Layout, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560102 ("we", "us").

What we collect

  • Name and mobile number; email if you give it.
  • On the Fleets and delivery riders and Apartments and offices pages only, a sales tool (Apollo) may recognise which business a visitor comes from, so we can offer them a fleet or society service. It is not used on booking pages.
  • Website analytics and advertising tags (Google and Meta) record which pages and ads brought you here and whether you sent a request. They do not receive your name, number or email.
  • Your location (a map pin and nearest area) only if you tap "Use my current location". You can remove it before sending.
  • Service details: area or address, bike brand, model, nickname and registration number, the service you choose, photos taken during inspection.
  • WhatsApp messages you send us about your booking, and your replies to rating and feedback requests.
  • If you apply to join our network as a garage or mechanic: your name, mobile number, garage name, area or address, the vehicles you work on and any note you add. We use this only to contact you about joining.
  • Payment status from Razorpay. We never see or store your card or UPI PIN.
  • Basic technical data (device, browser, approximate location from IP) for security and to prevent misuse.

Why we use it

To confirm and deliver your service, send booking updates and reminders on WhatsApp (only if you agree), process payments, give warranty support, prevent fraud and meet tax law. With your consent we may send offers; you can stop them anytime by replying STOP.

Automated replies

Some WhatsApp replies are written by an AI assistant trained on our services and prices. A team member reviews conversations and you can ask for a person at any time.

Who we share it with

Only as needed: the mechanic assigned to your job; the partner workshop that repairs your vehicle (see below); service providers who process data for us — Supabase (database), Cloudflare (hosting and security), Meta and AiSensy (WhatsApp messaging), Resend (email), Razorpay (payments), Anthropic (AI replies), Google (analytics, if enabled); and authorities where the law requires. We do not sell your data.

Partner workshops

When your vehicle goes to a partner workshop, or a partner garage or mechanic collects or repairs it, we share your first name, your mobile number and the pickup or service address with that garage or mechanic so it can reach you and find your vehicle. They see these only while your job is open and may not use them for anything else. We never share your email with them.

Our partner Axlio

Spare parts for your job are sourced from our partner Axlio, which is run separately from Mechanix Pro. We share the vehicle and part details with Axlio to source parts. We share your name, number or address with Axlio only where it is needed to deliver a part to you. If you visit axlio.in or use its services, Axlio's own privacy policy applies, not ours.

How long we keep it

Booking records and invoices for 8 years (tax law). Leads that do not become bookings are deleted or anonymised after 12 months. Chat logs for up to 24 months. Photos of your bike taken at pickup and return, 12 months.

Cookies and local storage

Our site saves your service build and small preferences (such as closing a banner) on your own device so the site works smoothly. Analytics and advertising tags are described above. You can clear this data in your browser settings at any time.

Children

Our services are for vehicle owners aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Security

Encrypted connections, access limited by role, bot and attack protection by Cloudflare, and audit logs of staff actions.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask to access, correct, update or delete your data, withdraw consent (withdrawing does not affect what we did before), and nominate another person to exercise these rights for you if you cannot. Write to our Grievance Officer below and we will respond within the time set by law. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above. For material changes we will tell you on the website or on WhatsApp.
