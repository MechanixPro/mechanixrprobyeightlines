What you get.

  • Hard to start

    Often a weak battery, a worn spark plug or a dirty air filter. We test and tell you which.

  • Low pickup or mileage

    Often a clogged air filter, old oil or a worn belt. A service usually brings it back.

  • Brakes weak or noisy

    Often worn shoes or pads. We check them and show you before replacing anything.

  • Puncture or worn tyre

    We come to you for a puncture, and check the tread and pressure on the spot.

  • Battery or self-start

    A battery health test shows if your scooty will start tomorrow.

  • Strange noise or vibration

    Chain, belt, suspension or bearings. We find the cause first and quote the fix.

Questions we get asked.

Can you repair my scooty at home?

Most scooty problems can be checked and many fixed at your door: starting, brakes, battery, tyres, belts and servicing. If a repair needs a workshop, we tell you before anything is moved, and the bike leaves only with your one-time code.

How much is a problem check?

A repair or problem check starts from ₹499, excluding GST. If you go ahead with the repair, the check fee is adjusted in your final bill. The repair itself is quoted after the check.

My scooty broke down on the road. What now?

Use our roadside help: share your location on WhatsApp and we send the nearest mechanic. Roadside visits start from ₹1,299.

Do you repair electric scooters too?

Yes, we check electric scooters for range, charging, brakes, tyres, lights and display problems.
