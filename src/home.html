Common questions.

How does it work?

Tell us your bike and what it needs, and pay a small confirmation fee ({{confirmrange}}, by engine size, adjusted in your final bill) to confirm your slot. When the mechanic arrives you share a one-time code, the mechanic checks the vehicle and sends an itemised quote, and work starts only after you approve it.

What does it cost?

Basic from {{text:basic}}, Standard from {{text:general}}, Full from {{text:full}}, excluding GST, for bikes up to 149cc. Bikes of 150 to 250cc and 251 to 500cc have their own prices. Pickup and drop is free in our serviceable area. Your exact quote comes on WhatsApp. To confirm your slot you pay {{confirmrule}}.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel more than 2 hours before your slot and your confirmation fee is refunded in full.

Which parts do you use?

OEM and aftermarket parts, sourced from our partner Axlio and labelled in your quote so you can choose. They are fitted only after you approve the quote.

How fast can you service my two wheeler in Bangalore?

A basic service can be done in as fast as 2 hours once the mechanic arrives. The time depends on the package and on what the mechanic finds, and we tell you before any extra work starts.

Do you do scooty repair at home?

Yes. Starting trouble, weak brakes, battery, tyres and servicing can be checked and often fixed at your door. A problem check starts from {{text:repair}}, and the repair is quoted after the check.

Are your garages verified?

Yes. Every garage and mechanic is visited, document-checked, trained and trial-run before taking customer jobs. That is what Mechanix Pro Certified means.

Do I get a service record?

Yes. Every job has a booking reference and a GST invoice, and your bike's history is kept against its registration number.

Is pickup and drop free?

Yes, pickup and drop is free in our serviceable area. The confirmation fee is adjusted in your final bill, and any check or repair is quoted first and added to that bill only after you approve it.

Is there a warranty?

Yes, {{days}}-day warranty on our service work. Parts carry a limited warranty on the terms of the part's manufacturer or supplier, shown on your invoice.

Who comes to my place?

A Mechanix Pro-certified mechanic. If your bike has to go to a workshop, it leaves only after you share a one-time code.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Bengaluru, every PIN code from 560001 to 560110, and we are open 24/7. Mechanics come to your home, office or society parking in areas including HSR Layout, Koramangala, BTM Layout, Bellandur, Electronic City, Marathahalli, Indiranagar, Whitefield, JP Nagar and Jayanagar. Check your PIN code on the areas page.

Is doorstep bike service safe?

Yes. Every visit is done by a Mechanix Pro-certified mechanic: before going live, each mechanic is visited by our team, has documents and tools checked, is trained on how we work and completes trial jobs. You get an itemised quote on WhatsApp before any work, the bike leaves only after you share a one-time code, and every job has a GST invoice and a 30-day service warranty.

What is included in a Standard service?

The Standard service ({{price:general}} for bikes up to 149cc, excluding GST) covers 24 checkpoints across the engine and filters, brakes, clutch and drive, tyres and suspension, electricals, and body and safety. It includes an engine oil level and condition check, air filter cleaning, spark plug cleaning, brake cleaning and adjustment, clutch and chain adjustment, chain lubrication, and tyre, battery and light checks.
