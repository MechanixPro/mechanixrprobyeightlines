How the {{fee:advance}} checkup and quote fee works

New customers pay only {{fee:newfee}} to lock a slot, so we can serve genuine customers. Returning customers pay {{fee:advance}}. Both are adjusted in your final bill and refunded on the same cancellation rule.

  1. You approve the quote first. Nothing is charged while you are still deciding.
  2. The fee locks your slot. It pays for the mechanic's visit and the checkup that makes your quote.
  3. Go ahead and it comes off your bill. The fee is adjusted in your final bill if you do the service.
  4. Cancel in time and it is refunded. More than 2 hours before your slot, you get all of it back. See the refund policy.
