Pro Check 20.

Before your quote, your mechanic or partner garage checks 20 points on your bike and finishes with a test ride. They record each result in the app, and you see the list on your tracking page.

The 20 points

Brakes

  • Front brake
  • Rear brake
  • Brake cable or fluid level

Tyres and wheels

  • Front tyre condition
  • Rear tyre condition
  • Tyre pressure

Engine

  • Engine oil level
  • Engine idle
  • Air filter
  • Spark plug

Electrical

  • Battery
  • Headlight and indicators
  • Horn

Drive

  • Chain tension and lubrication
  • Clutch
  • Throttle

Frame

  • Suspension and fork
  • Stands
  • Nuts and bolts tightened

Leaks and noise

  • Any leak or unusual noise

Then a test ride

After the 20 points, the mechanic records a short test ride: done and all fine, done with something to note, or not done with the reason (no safe road nearby, you asked us not to, or the bike cannot be ridden). Only a rider with a valid licence rides your bike, and only on safe nearby roads.

What you see

Your private tracking page shows how many points were OK and how many need attention, each point with a tick or an alert and the mechanic's note, and the test ride result. If something needs attention, it appears in your itemised quote, and nothing starts until you say yes.

Good to know

  • Pro Check 20 is for bikes and scooters. Electric two-wheelers skip the points that do not apply, marked Skipped.
  • It is a check, not a repair. Any work is quoted first.
