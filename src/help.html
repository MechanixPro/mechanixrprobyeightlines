How do I confirm my booking?

Approve the quote on WhatsApp and we confirm a slot. We then send a secure Razorpay link to pay the {{fee:advance}} checkup and quote fee ({{fee:newfee}} for new customers). It confirms your booking and is adjusted in your final bill if you go ahead with the service.

Most routine services (oil, filters, chain, brakes, battery, tyres) can be done at your doorstep. If your problem needs workshop tools, our expert tells you on WhatsApp and we pick up the bike only after you share a one-time code.
