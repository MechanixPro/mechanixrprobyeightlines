Chapter 1

It started with a question.

Every rider knows the routine. Take the day off, ride to a workshop, wait, and hope the bill matches the work. We asked a simpler thing: what if the service came to you, and you could see and approve everything before it began?

A service you can see, approve and pay for, without leaving home.The idea behind Mechanix Pro
Chapter 2

The three worries.

Talk to riders and the same three worries come up. We built around each one.

  • "Will the bill change?"

    You get an itemised quote first. Extra work needs a new approval. Nothing starts until you say yes.

  • "Who has my bike?"

    You get the booking ID, the mechanic's name and a private tracking link. A one-time code at the door protects your vehicle.

  • "What was actually done?"

    Photos at pickup, when work is done and at delivery. A Pro Check on 20 points and a test ride. A GST bill.
