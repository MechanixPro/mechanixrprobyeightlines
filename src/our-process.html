How we keep your bike safe.

Nine steps, from your booking to the finished job. You can see each one on your tracking page.

  1. STEP 1You book and pay a small confirmation fee

    The fee is {{confirmrange}} by engine size, GST included. It is adjusted in your final bill.

  2. STEP 2You get your booking ID, tracking link and code

    You get a booking ID, a private tracking link and a 4-digit code by email and WhatsApp.

  3. STEP 3We confirm your slot and assign a mechanic

    We confirm your slot. A checked mechanic or partner garage is assigned to your job.

  4. STEP 4They tell you when they are on the way

    You are told when they are on the way and when they are near. You can see it on your tracking page.

  5. STEP 5At the door you share your one-time code

    Work cannot start without it.

  6. STEP 6They inspect and send you an itemised quote

    Nothing starts until you say yes, on your tracking page or by phone. First they run the Pro Check 20 and a test ride.

  7. STEP 7Photos at pickup, when work is done and at delivery

    Replaced parts are shown to you.

  8. STEP 8You pay the final amount online

    Pay by QR or link. Cash or UPI only if you chose to inspect first. You always get a bill.

  9. STEP 9Your repair work is covered for {{days}} days, and you can rate us

    Our service work is covered for {{days}} days from the service date. If the same work fails in that time, we redo it free. Parts carry a limited warranty on the terms of the part's manufacturer or supplier.

Who works on your bike

  • Partners and mechanics send their documents and photos.
  • We check them before they take jobs.
  • We do not publish their documents.

Your money

  • The confirmation fee is adjusted in your final bill.
  • A {{visitcharge}} visiting charge applies to any doorstep visit. If you decline to continue once a technician is assigned, it is payable and your confirmation fee counts towards it. If you go ahead with the service, no visiting charge is added to your bill. On your first booking with us, the visiting charge is waived and your confirmation fee follows the cancellation rules.
  • Prices are shown before work.
  • No work without your OK.
  • Read our refund policy.

Your data

  • The mechanic or partner garage on your job gets your first name, mobile number and the pickup or service address, so they can reach you and find your vehicle.
  • They see these only while your job is open.
  • We never share your email with them.
  • Details are in our privacy policy.

If something goes wrong

  • Call us or message us on WhatsApp.
  • Track your job.
  • Read our terms and refund policy.
