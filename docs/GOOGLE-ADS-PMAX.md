# Google Ads Performance Max pack: ₹5,000 test

Performance Max (PMax) shows ads across Search, YouTube, Display, Gmail and Discover, and Google decides where. It works best with plenty of conversions. With ₹5,000 it may not learn enough, so check it after 2 weeks (see the end).

Create it **paused**. Daily budget **₹165**.

## 1. Basics
- Objective: **Leads**. Conversion goals: only **Lead**.
- Campaign type: **Performance Max**. Name: `Bike service Bengaluru (PMax)`
- Bidding: **Conversions** (Maximize conversions). Leave the target cost per action empty.
- Customer acquisition: off.

## 2. Settings
- Location: **Bengaluru, Karnataka, India**, option **Presence**.
- Language: English. Ad schedule: every day, 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM.
- **Final URL expansion: OFF** (keeps traffic on the landing page).
- **Automatically created assets: OFF** (all text, image and video options).
- Final URL: `https://mechanixpro.in/offers/bike-service/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=pmax&utm_campaign=bike-service-pmax`

## 5. After creating it
- Account settings, **Negative keywords**: add a list with: free, jobs, job, course, training, spare parts wholesale, second hand, used bike, for sale, rent, rental, insurance, mechanic job, diy, how to, youtube, pdf, tyre shop, showroom.
- Switch off every recommendation that auto-applies.

## 6. Check before you Enable
- [ ] Billing is set up and the Lead conversion exists
- [ ] The old "Purchase" conversions are Secondary or removed
- [ ] Budget is ₹165 a day and the campaign is **paused** until you are ready
- [ ] Someone answers WhatsApp 7 AM to 9 PM within minutes

## 7. Judge it fairly
- Wait 7 to 10 days before changing anything. PMax needs time to learn.
- Look at **Insights** and the **Channels** report. If most of the spend goes to Display or YouTube and few leads come in, switch to a Search campaign with the pack in `docs/GOOGLE-ADS-CAMPAIGN.md`.
- Judge by cost per completed job in the admin, not by clicks.
