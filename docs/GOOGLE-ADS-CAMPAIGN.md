## 1. Start the campaign
Campaigns, then **+ New campaign**.
- Objective: **Leads** (or "Create a campaign without a goal's guidance")
- Conversion goal: tick only **Lead**, untick everything else
- Campaign type: **Search**
- Results you want: **Website visits** (enter `https://mechanixpro.in/offers/bike-service/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=bike-service`)
- Campaign name: `Bike service Bengaluru (search)`

- Bidding: **Clicks**, then tick **Set a maximum cost per click bid limit** and enter **₹30** (a starting guess; raise it in small steps if there are no impressions after 2 days)

- Locations: **Bengaluru** (search "Bengaluru, Karnataka, India"), then under Location options choose **Presence: People in or regularly in your targeted locations**
- Languages: **English**
- Ad schedule: Monday to Sunday, **7:00 AM to 9:00 PM**
- Turn **off** "Automatically created assets", "Final URL expansion" and "AI Max" if offered
