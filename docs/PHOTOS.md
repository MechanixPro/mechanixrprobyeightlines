# Adding your own photos

1. Put photos in `~/Downloads/mechanixpro-photos/` (JPG, PNG or iPhone HEIC).
2. Tell Claude "photos added". Claude converts them with `scripts/add_photo.sh` and places them on the site.
3. Wanted shots: mechanic at a customer's door, inspection with the customer, a finished bike, the team, the tools or van, a chain or brake check. Use your own mechanics and work only. Avoid customer faces and number plates.
