3.2 Changing an invoice: same invoice or a new one Mock-up

When something changes after an invoice is issued, you choose one of two clear paths:

Update this invoice

Use for a correction (wrong amount, missing item, wrong vehicle number). The invoice keeps its number and gets a revision mark, for example INV-MP-53020C-R2. The old version stays in the register but is marked "replaced".

Create a separate invoice

Use for extra work done later, or when part of the bill is for a different customer or company. It gets its own number, for example INV-MP-53020C-B, and its own total. The booking shows all of its invoices together with one overall paid and balance figure.

Every change asks for a short reason and is recorded, so nothing is ever lost. Deleted invoices are hidden, not erased, and can be restored.

5.2 Draft terms printed on the card and the verify page

  • Validity: 30 days from the date the service was done (matches the current "30-day service warranty" on the site).
  • Covers: workmanship on the service performed. Parts carry the part maker's limited warranty, as labelled on the invoice.
  • Does not cover: accidents, misuse, water damage, wear parts used up in normal riding, or work done by anyone else after our service.
  • To claim: message the number on the card with the booking number and a photo of the issue.
These terms are draft wording. Please confirm or edit them before they go on a customer-facing card; the final wording should be reviewed against your invoice terms.

When it is sent: once, automatically, when the booking's paid amount equals its total. If the amount changes later (for example extra work), the card is not re-sent unless you choose to resend it.

9. Assumptions, risks and open points

  • Handwriting: a few lines in the notes were hard to read. We read them as set out above. Please correct anything we got wrong.
  • WhatsApp approvals: the warranty card and the mechanic payment message are sent as WhatsApp template messages. Meta must approve each template first, which can take a day or more and is outside our control. Meta business verification also helps.
  • Price change: moving to prices excluding GST changes what customers are charged. We will not release phase 3 until you approve the price table.
  • Accounting records: invoices are never erased. Edits create revisions; deletes hide and can be restored.
  • Brand names in media: the background bike and the oil bottles show other companies' brands; used as decoration and job footage only.
  • Out of scope now: the dashboard and bookings screens (you marked them "all good"), AiSensy and ads.
