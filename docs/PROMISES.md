# Mechanix Pro promises: names, exact rules, and what the system must do

Owner decisions 2026-11-09: Shield up to Rs 5,000 paid by the company; Live = photo and short-video updates plus an on-request video call. A promise goes on the website only when its system part is live and the terms say the same thing.

| Name | Promise in one line | Exact rule | System must | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pro Check 20** | A 20-point check on every service | The mechanic or garage ticks 20 points in the app. The customer sees the list with each result on the tracking page | Checklist step in mechanic and garage apps, saved per job, shown on tracking page | Not built |
| **Mechanix Live** | Watch your service from your phone | Photos and short clips at each step on the tracking page. Customer can tap Video call to ask the mechanic to show the work live on WhatsApp video | Short-video upload (15 s) in the apps, clip gallery and a Video call button on the tracking page | Not built |
| **Mechanix Shield** | Up to Rs 5,000 if your vehicle is damaged in our care | Valid claim, reported within 48 hours of delivery, with pickup and delivery photos as evidence, paid by the company up to Rs 5,000 per job. Exclusions: existing damage shown in pickup photos, wear and tear, normal parts failure, theft by the customer's own acts | Claim form on tracking page, Crew claim queue with photos, approve and pay record, recovery from the garage or mechanic when at fault | Not built |
| **Genuine Parts Promise** | Parts are genuine or equivalent quality, and you see which | Each part in the quote and invoice is marked Genuine (OEM) or Equivalent (OES or aftermarket). Parts marked Genuine come from the maker's authorised channel and are shown with the bill. We never charge for a new part and fit a used one | Part type field on quote lines, shown on quote, invoice and tracking page | Not built |
| **Redo Promise** | Free redo if our repair work fails | If the same repair work fails within 30 days of the service date, we redo it free. Parts follow the maker's warranty | Redo request button on tracking page and in the warranty page, Crew redo queue, partner must redo within 3 working days (already in the agreements) | Warranty page exists, redo flow to add |
| **Today Slots** | Book a slot today when there is room | Today shows as a choice when a mechanic is free before the cut-off time. Otherwise the earliest slot is shown | Booking form reads open slots, hides today after cut-off | To check |
| **PIN Check** | Check your PIN before you book | We cover the PIN codes listed. A PIN not listed gets a message us answer | Already live | Live |

Words we do not use: guaranteed, 100%, zero risk, insured (Shield is a company promise, not insurance), police verified.

## Order of build
1. Pro Check 20 (all apps and tracking page)
2. Redo Promise (rename, button, queue)
3. Genuine Parts Promise (quote line field)
4. Mechanix Shield (claim flow, terms, agreements)
5. Mechanix Live (clips, video call button)
6. Today Slots (check, then adjust)
Website copy for each goes live with its system part. Terms, refund policy and both partner agreements are updated in the same release as Shield and Genuine Parts.
