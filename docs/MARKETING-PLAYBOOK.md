# Mechanix Pro marketing playbook (the Fastlane method, run in-house)

Written 2026-11-04. Fastlane (usefastlane.ai) sells an AI agent that loops: learn the brand, research the market, create, launch, learn. We run the same loop ourselves, adapted to a Bengaluru doorstep service that gets its leads as WhatsApp chats. No tool fee. Every post and every rupee needs the owner's yes.

## The loop

| Step | Fastlane does | We do |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Learn the brand | Reads the website for product, colours, tone | Done: navy `#14295A`, ember `#F2801F`, "Quote first, pay after you say yes", `src/site.json` prices |
| 2. Research | Competitor ads, posts, reviews | Weekly look at Ride N Repair, DriveX, ReadyAssist, BroMechanic, Bikerz Hub, Ride-Xpert: offer, H1, proof, price shown |
| 3. Create | Ads, short videos, AI creators | 4–6 ads per round in one Meta ad set, 3 Reels a week from real footage |
| 4. Launch | Bulk Meta launch, scheduled posts | Owner approves, then we publish. Existing posts go into the WhatsApp ad set as ads |
| 5. Learn | Reads CPA and ROAS, plans the next batch | Daily: leads, cost per chat, cost per booking (not just per lead), by ad and by area |

## What we add (what Fastlane does not do for us)

- **WhatsApp-first.** Goal is a chat, not a click. Cost per chat and cost per booking are the numbers that matter. About 12% of leads booked on 3 Nov, so a lead at ₹53 is about ₹440 per booking.
- **Real footage, not AI people.** Real mechanics, real bikes, real doorsteps. No AI "customers", no invented reviews, no rented accounts.
- **Free local channels.** Apartment and society partners ("1,300+ gated communities"), a WhatsApp message to past customers, Google Business Profile, referral asks, fleet and delivery-rider partners.
- **Booking-rate work.** The desk calling back unanswered leads lowers every cost figure for free.

## Rules for every creative

- Only these claims: "10,000+ services done" and "1,300+ gated communities trust us". No ratings, review counts or other numbers.
- No prices on images. Prices live on the site (`src/site.json`) and in the WhatsApp quote.
- No time-limited offers on posts we keep boosting (the Dussehra ₹499 post is expired).
- Checkpoint counts must match the site: Basic 15, Standard 24. Posts saying "20-point check" or "wash" in Basic do not match the site.
- No competitor logos or copied ads.

Actual spend was not readable in the tool, so the cost column is the budget divided by chats. Chats started are not all real leads. Lesson: one post made 71 chats and its copy made 4. Results depend on the post, so test several before spending big.

## Weekly rhythm

- **Mon:** read last week's numbers, pick winners and losers, write 4–6 new ads.
- **Tue to Thu:** owner approves, ads and Reels go live, desk calls back every unanswered lead.
- **Daily 6 PM:** lead report: chats, leads, bookings, cost per chat, cost per booking, by ad.
- **Fri:** pause ads with no chats after ₹500, move budget to the winners.
- **Raise budget in steps of 20–30% at a time**, only after the owner says yes.
