## Rules for the week
- Day 1 check at 11 AM and 6 PM: leads in the desk, cost per lead, Google search terms.
- Pause any ad that spends ₹600 with no lead. Keep the best two ads on each platform.
- Day 4: move ₹300 a day from the weakest campaign to the best one. Add the 7-day website visitors retargeting ad set at ₹100 a day.
- Planning assumptions, not promises: Google clicks around ₹30 to ₹50, Meta clicks around ₹6 to ₹15, 8 to 12% of site visitors send a request. Judge by cost per real lead, not clicks.
- Every lead gets a call within 5 minutes. The desk emails the team at once, and the supervisor email alert fires after 15 minutes.
