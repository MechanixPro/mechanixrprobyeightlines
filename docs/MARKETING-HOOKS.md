# Hooks for genuine leads (4 Nov 2026)

For owner approval. Nothing here is posted. These are proven hook *formats* (pattern interrupt, POV, myth-busting, checklist, "stop scrolling if", mechanic reacts, before/after), not live trend data: I have not pulled this week's trending audio or topics. Timely angle used: Diwali week (Sun 8 Nov), with no offers.

## Rules for every hook

- Only the claims "10,000+ services done" and "1,300+ gated communities trust us" (bike lines only; never on car or parts). No ratings, review counts or other numbers.
- Allowed facts: quote first and pay after you say OK; OTP before the bike leaves; 30-day service warranty; GST invoice; open 24/7 for requests; a basic service can be done in as fast as 2 hours once the mechanic arrives. Do not promise an arrival time for roadside.
- No prices on images. No fake people vouching for us. No "limited time" unless there is a real, dated offer.

## What makes a lead genuine (not just a chat)

1. **Name the area or model in the hook.** "HSR Layout riders" filters out people outside our areas before they chat.
2. **Make the first message ask for details.** A generic "Chat with us" invites "price?" only. The opening WhatsApp message should ask for the model and the area (templates below).
3. **Promise the quote, not the price.** "Send your bike model, get an itemised quote" attracts people who want the work, not just a number.
4. **Ask for one thing.** A hook that ends with "Send your model and area" beats one with three options.
5. **Judge hooks on bookings.** Keep a hook only if its chats turn into bookings, not just chats.

## WhatsApp opening messages (set in Meta's conversation settings, one per line)

- Bike: "Hi Mechanix Pro, I need a bike service. Bike model: __ Area or PIN: __ Preferred day: __"

The car ad published today uses Meta's default welcome message ("Hi! Please let us know how we can help you."). Replacing it with the car message above is the first lead-quality fix to make.

## How to test

- 4 to 6 hooks per ad set, one idea each. Pause any hook with no chats after ₹500 of spend.
- Count a hook as a winner only when its chats become bookings. Report cost per chat and cost per booking by hook every day.
- Reels: post the same footage with 2 different hooks on different days and compare saves and shares.
