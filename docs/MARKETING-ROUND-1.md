| Competitor | Offer shown | Warranty | Speed claim | Proof shown | How to book |

| BroMechanic | General ₹599, Premium ₹999 | 15 days | No time | 22-point check, no counts | Web, WhatsApp, phone |
| Bikerz Hub | General ₹999 | 30-day workmanship | 30–60 min arrival | 10,000+ motorcycles, 35-point check | WhatsApp, phone, web. Closed Sundays. Some pages unfinished |
| Ride-Xpert | Full ₹999 (up to 125cc) | None | Callback in 15 min | 30,000+ bikes, 4.8★ (scale unclear) | Web form (opens email), phone, WhatsApp |

**Openings for Mechanix Pro**
1. **WhatsApp-first.** Only 3 of the 6 offer WhatsApp. The biggest, Ride N Repair, lists app and phone only.
2. **Quote first, pay after you say OK, and OTP handover.** None of the six pages led with either.
3. **Open 24/7 for requests.** Others have limited hours; Bikerz Hub is closed Sundays.
4. **30-day warranty** matches Ride N Repair and Bikerz Hub and beats BroMechanic's 15 days.
5. **Society angle:** "1,300+ gated communities trust us" is a claim none of them make.

**Where we cannot compete**
- **Ratings and customer counts.** Ride N Repair shows 4.8★ and 2,00,000+. We use only the two confirmed claims.
- **Price.** Our Basic is ₹499 before GST (about ₹589 with 18% GST). Ride N Repair shows "from ₹450" and we cannot tell if that includes GST. Compete on trust and ease, not on price.
- **Referral offer.** Only Ride N Repair has one (₹500 each). That costs money, so it is the owner's decision.

| Meta (India) | **2 ads only** | Same copy twice: "Bike service without the workshop hassle!", a bullet list (service, repairs, battery, tyre and brake) and a Book now button to ridenrepair.com. One says Hyderabad, one says Mumbai. Running since 16 and 17 Sep. **None for Bengaluru.** |
| Google (India) | **about 300 ads** | Text search ads such as "Doorstep Car Repair" with chips "Car Service near Me" and "Car Mechanic at Your Home". Video ads (8 of the first 80 loaded) with "BOOK NOW", a phone number and the website. |

**What this means**
1. Their paid push is Google and YouTube, car-heavy and call-led. Meta is almost unused, and not at all in Bengaluru, so **WhatsApp-first ads on Meta in Bengaluru face no direct rival from them.**
2. They compete hard for "car service at home" searches. Our car service page and Axlio's car help sit in that gap, so a car search ad is worth testing.
3. Their phone number sits in the video. Ours is WhatsApp, so the button should read Send message.
