How it works.

From your first message to the finished service, with the questions people ask most.

What happens at your doorstep.

No waiting rooms. No surprises on the bill.

  1. 1

    Build and send

    Pick your model, tell us what it needs, and send it to us on WhatsApp in one tap.

  2. 2

    Expert checks and quotes

    Our expert calls or messages you, confirms what is needed and whether it can be done at home, then sends your quote on WhatsApp.

  3. 3

    You approve, we book

    Say yes on WhatsApp and we lock your slot. The ₹349 checkup and quote fee confirms your booking and is adjusted in your final bill if you go ahead with the service. New customers pay only ₹99.

  4. 4

    We service your bike

    The mechanic arrives in your slot and shows you what your bike needs. Extra work comes as an itemised estimate and starts only after your OK. We use OEM and aftermarket parts.

  5. 5

    Invoice and warranty

    You get a GST invoice and a 30-day service warranty. Parts carry a limited warranty on the terms of the part's manufacturer or supplier, shown on your invoice.

Questions, answered.

Which parts of Bengaluru do you serve?

All of Bengaluru. We come to every PIN code from 560001 to 560110, including HSR Layout, Koramangala, Indiranagar, Whitefield, Jayanagar, JP Nagar, Hebbal and Electronic City. Type your PIN code on the home page to check it.

How do I confirm my booking?

Approve the quote on WhatsApp and we confirm a slot. We then send a secure Razorpay link to pay the ₹349 checkup and quote fee (₹99 for new customers). It confirms your booking and is adjusted in your final bill if you go ahead with the service.

Can my bike be serviced at home?

Most routine services (oil, filters, chain, brakes, battery, tyres) can be done at your doorstep. If your problem needs workshop tools, our expert tells you on WhatsApp and we pick up the bike only after you share a one-time code.

How does the quote work?

Send us your build on WhatsApp. An expert calls or messages you, asks what is wrong, and sends an itemised quote. Nothing starts until you approve it.

What if more work is needed?

The mechanic shows you what is wrong and sends an itemised estimate. Nothing extra is done or charged unless you approve it.

Which bikes do you service?

All major brands: Honda, Hero, TVS, Bajaj, Royal Enfield, Yamaha, Suzuki, KTM and electric scooters for tyres, brakes and general checks.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel free up to 2 hours before your slot and the ₹349 checkup and quote fee is refunded. See our refund policy.

Is there a warranty?

Our service work is covered for 30 days from the service date. Parts also carry the manufacturer's warranty.
