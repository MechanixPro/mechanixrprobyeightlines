How it works.

From your first message to the finished service, with the questions people ask most.

What happens at your doorstep.

No waiting rooms. No surprises on the bill.

Book and confirm

Pick your vehicle, your PIN code and a time on this website, or ask our team on a call to send you the booking link. You pay a small confirmation fee, ₹49 to ₹99 depending on your vehicle's engine size, shown before you pay and adjusted in your final bill, and it confirms your booking.

Your booking ID and tracking

We send your booking ID, a one-time code and a tracking link by email and WhatsApp. We assign a mechanic, and you can follow them on the way.

Share your code when they arrive

Give the one-time code to the mechanic only when they arrive. It starts the job and protects your vehicle, whether it is repaired at your door or picked up.

Itemised quote, you approve

The mechanic checks your vehicle and we send an itemised quote with an estimated delivery time. Work starts only after you approve it. Extra work needs a new approval. We use OEM and aftermarket parts.

Invoice and warranty

You get a GST invoice and a 30-day service warranty. Parts carry a limited warranty on the terms of the part's manufacturer or supplier, shown on your invoice.

Questions, answered.

Which parts of Bengaluru do you serve?

All of Bengaluru. We come to every PIN code from 560001 to 560110, including HSR Layout, Koramangala, Indiranagar, Whitefield, Jayanagar, JP Nagar, Hebbal and Electronic City. Type your PIN code on the home page to check it.

How do I confirm my booking?

Book on this website or on a call, and pay a small confirmation fee, ₹49 to ₹99 depending on your vehicle's engine size, shown before you pay and adjusted in your final bill. That confirms your booking, and we send your booking ID, a one-time code and a tracking link by email and WhatsApp. The mechanic checks your vehicle and sends an itemised quote that you approve before any work starts.

Can my bike be serviced at home?

Most routine services (oil, filters, chain, brakes, battery, tyres) can be done at your doorstep by a Mechanix Pro-certified mechanic. If your problem needs workshop tools, our expert tells you on WhatsApp and we pick up the bike only after you share a one-time code.

How does the quote work?

Send us your build on WhatsApp. An expert calls or messages you, asks what is wrong, and sends an itemised quote. Nothing starts until you approve it.

What if more work is needed?

The mechanic shows you what is wrong and sends an itemised estimate. Nothing extra is done or charged unless you approve it.

Which bikes do you service?

All major brands: Honda, Hero, TVS, Bajaj, Royal Enfield, Yamaha, Suzuki, KTM and electric scooters for tyres, brakes and general checks.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel free up to 2 hours before your slot and your confirmation fee is refunded in full. See our refund policy.

Is there a warranty?

Our service work is covered for 30 days from the service date. Parts carry a limited warranty on the terms of the part's manufacturer or supplier, shown on your invoice. If the same repair work fails within 30 days, we redo it free: this is our Redo Promise, and you can ask for it from your tracking page.

What if my vehicle is damaged while you have it?

Mechanix Shield: Mechanix Pro pays up to Rs 5,000 per job for a valid claim. Report it from your tracking page within 48 hours of delivery. We check it against the photos taken at pickup and delivery. It does not cover damage already shown in the pickup photos, normal wear or normal parts failure. It is a promise by the company, not an insurance policy.

Are the parts genuine?

Every part on your quote and invoice is marked Genuine (from the maker's authorised channel) or Equivalent (equivalent quality), so you can see which you are paying for. We never charge for a new part and fit a used one.

Can I watch my service?

Mechanix Live: your mechanic or partner garage shares photos and short clips while the work goes on, and you see them on your tracking page. You can also ask for a WhatsApp video call from that page while the job is on.

Can I book for today?

Yes, when a time window is still ahead. Today is offered while a window starts at least two hours from now. After that, the earliest slot is tomorrow.
