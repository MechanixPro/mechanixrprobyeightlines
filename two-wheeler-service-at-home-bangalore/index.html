Questions we get asked.

Do you do two wheeler service at home in Bangalore?

Yes. Our mechanics come to your home, office or society parking across Bangalore (Bengaluru), for bikes and scooters, petrol and electric.

How fast can a service be done?

A basic service can be done in as fast as 2 hours once the mechanic arrives. The time depends on the package and on what the mechanic finds, and we tell you before any extra work starts.

What does it cost?

Basic from ₹499, Standard from ₹799, Full from ₹1,299 for bikes up to 149cc, GST included. Bikes of 150 to 250cc and 251 to 500cc have their own prices. Your exact quote comes on WhatsApp first.

Which bikes do you service?

Scooters, commuter motorcycles, Royal Enfields, performance bikes and electric scooters. Not sure about yours? Message us the model and we confirm.
