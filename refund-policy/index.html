Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026.

Cancelling a booking

  • More than 2 hours before your slot: free. Your confirmation fee is refunded in full.
  • Less than 2 hours before, or after the mechanic has left: the confirmation fee covers the visit and is not refunded.
  • Emergency (SOS) visits: cancellable free until a mechanic is assigned.

Visiting charge

A visiting charge of ₹349 applies to any doorstep visit. It covers the technician's travel and time to your place. The confirmation fee is separate: it confirms your slot. If, once a technician has been assigned, you decline to continue with the service or the quote, the ₹349 visiting charge is payable and the confirmation fee you have already paid counts towards it. If you go ahead with the service, no visiting charge is added to your bill.

If we cancel

If we cannot reach you in your slot, we offer another slot or a full refund — your choice.

Service issues

If something we fixed fails within 30 days, we redo that work free (the Redo Promise: ask from your tracking page or message us with your booking ID). If we cannot fix it, we refund the charge for that item. Parts are covered by the limited warranty of the part's manufacturer or supplier, as shown on your invoice.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go to your original payment method, through whichever of Razorpay or Cashfree Payments took your payment, within 5–7 working days of approval.

Contact

Write to hello@mechanixpro.in or message us on WhatsApp with your booking reference.
