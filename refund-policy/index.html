Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026.

Cancelling a booking

  • More than 2 hours before your slot: free. Your ₹349 checkup and quote fee is refunded in full.
  • Less than 2 hours before, or after the mechanic has left: the ₹349 checkup and quote fee covers the visit and is not refunded.
  • Emergency (SOS) visits: cancellable free until a mechanic is assigned.

If we cancel

If we cannot reach you in your slot, we offer another slot or a full refund — your choice.

Service issues

If something we fixed fails within 30 days, we redo that work free. If we cannot fix it, we refund the charge for that item.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go to your original payment method through Razorpay within 5–7 working days of approval.

Contact

Write to hello@mechanixpro.in or message us on WhatsApp with your booking reference.
