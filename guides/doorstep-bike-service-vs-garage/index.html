Doorstep bike service or a garage: which is better?

Both have a place. This is a fair look at what each is good at, so you can choose for your bike and your day.

Time and effort

A garage visit usually means riding in, waiting or arranging a ride home, and collecting the bike later. A doorstep service happens where the bike is parked, at home or at your office, usually in 60 to 90 minutes, so you lose far less of your day.

Knowing the price first

With Mechanix Pro you get a quote on WhatsApp before anything starts, and parts are added only after you approve. Whatever you choose, ask for the price in writing before the work begins.

Parts and warranty

We use OEM and aftermarket parts and give a 30-day warranty on the work. Ask any garage the same two questions: which parts will be used, and what is guaranteed.

What a garage can still do better

Some jobs need a lift, special tools or a longer stay, such as major engine work. If a job cannot be done properly at your doorstep, we tell you before starting, and you can then take it to a workshop.

Which should you pick?

For regular services, brakes, chain care, oil changes and routine checks, doorstep service saves time. For a major repair, a workshop may be the right place. When in doubt, send us your bike's problem on WhatsApp and we will say honestly what can be done at home.

Common questions

Is doorstep bike service as good as a garage?

For routine services and common checks, yes, when the mechanic is certified and uses proper parts. For major engine work, a workshop may be the better place, and we say so before starting.

Do I pay before the work?

You approve the quote first. To hold your slot you pay a small confirmation fee, ₹49 to ₹99 depending on your vehicle's engine size, shown before you pay and adjusted in your final bill.
