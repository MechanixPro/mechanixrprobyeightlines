How often should you service your bike or scooter?

The honest answer is: as often as your owner's manual says, and sooner if your riding is hard on the bike. Here is how to think about it in Bengaluru.

Start with the owner's manual

Every maker publishes a service schedule, usually by kilometres or by months, whichever comes first. Many commuter bikes and scooters ask for a service every few thousand kilometres or every few months. The exact number depends on your model, so check the manual or the service booklet and follow it.

Why Bengaluru riding can need more care

Stop-start traffic, long idling at signals, potholes and the monsoon all add wear that the kilometre count does not show. A bike that does short, crowded trips every day can need attention earlier than one that cruises on open roads.

Signs your bike needs a service sooner

  • Brakes that feel soft, noisy or slow to bite
  • A chain that is loose, noisy or dry
  • Hard starting, a rough idle or weaker pickup
  • A clutch that slips or feels heavy
  • Engine oil that is dark, low or past its due date
  • A battery that struggles on the self-start

If you notice any of these, get the bike checked rather than waiting for the next due date.

Electric scooters and bikes

An electric scooter has no engine oil to change, but it still needs regular checks: brakes, tyres, suspension, lights, battery health and connectors, and how it charges. If the range drops or charging stops early, have it looked at.

Common questions

How often should I service my bike?

Follow the schedule in your owner's manual, which is usually by kilometres or months, whichever comes first. Service sooner if the brakes, chain, clutch or starting feel off.

Do electric scooters need servicing?

Yes. There is no engine oil, but brakes, tyres, suspension, lights, battery health and charging still need regular checks.
