What a Mechanix Pro garage is.

A garage or mechanic in our network that has been visited, document-checked, trained on our way of working and trial-run before taking customer jobs.

When a bike must go to a workshop, it goes to a Mechanix Pro Certified garage. The repair is quoted first and starts only after the customer approves.

  • Visited and document-checked
  • Trained on quote first and honest findings
  • Bike leaves and returns only with the customer's one-time code
  • Photos at pickup and at return

What you get.

  • Visited in person

    Our team visits the workshop, sees the bays, tools and how the work is done.

  • Documents checked

    Identity and business documents are verified before anyone goes live.

  • Trained our way

    A short training on how Mechanix Pro works: quote first, approval before extra work, honest findings.

  • Trial jobs first

    New partners start with a few supervised jobs before they take regular bookings.

  • Rated by customers

    After each job we ask the customer to rate it. Low ratings are looked at by our team.

  • OTP-safe handover

    If a bike must go to a workshop, it leaves and returns only with your one-time code.
