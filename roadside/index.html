Roadside questions.

How much does roadside help cost in Bengaluru?

A roadside emergency visit is ₹1,299 and a puncture is ₹599, excluding GST. The visit covers the diagnosis and quick roadside help. Any repair beyond that is quoted first, and no major repair starts without your approval.

What can the mechanic fix at the roadside?

Breakdown diagnosis, puncture help, battery jump-start, starting problems, fuel-related help and minor on-the-spot repairs. If the bike cannot be fixed where it is, emergency towing support is arranged if required, and the repair is quoted before any work starts.

How fast can roadside help reach me?

We send the nearest available mechanic as soon as you share your live location on WhatsApp. Arrival time depends on traffic and where you are, so it is not guaranteed. We take requests 24/7 across our service areas in Bengaluru.

Is roadside help available 24/7?

Yes, we take roadside requests 24/7. Mechanic availability and response depend on the time and your location. We cannot attend if the location is unsafe, so move off the road first if you can.
