Mechanix Pro on your phone.

Book, track your job and see your photos and bill from your home screen. There is no store download. You add it from your browser in two taps, and it opens like an app.

Customers

Add it to your home screen

Open mechanixpro.in in your browser, then:

  1. iPhone: tap the Share button in Safari, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android: tap the browser menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Open Mechanix Pro from your home screen. If you have a recent booking, it opens your tracking page.

Add it from your tracking page to open that page by default.

Garages and mechanics

Your partner app

Partners get a private link and a PIN when they are approved. The app gives you new jobs with an alert, the address with a Navigate button, one step per screen, photos, payment and your statement.

  1. Apply on the Join page and upload your documents from your phone.
  2. When approved, open your private link and enter your PIN.
  3. Add the app to your home screen the same way as above.
