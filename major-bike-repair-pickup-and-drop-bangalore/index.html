  • Free pickup and drop

How it works.

  1. 1Book a problem check, or message us with what is wrong.
  2. 2The mechanic checks your bike at your door and sends a quote on WhatsApp.
  3. 3If it needs a garage, you approve, and we pick it up free with your one-time code.
  4. 4The Mechanix Pro Certified garage repairs it, and we keep you posted on WhatsApp.
  5. 5We bring it back, you share your code, and you get your invoice and warranty.

Questions we get asked.

What counts as a major repair?

Jobs that usually need a workshop, such as engine, clutch, gearbox, suspension or electrical faults. The mechanic tells you after the check whether your bike needs a garage.

Is pickup and drop really free?

Yes, pickup and drop is free within our serviceable area in Bangalore. Spare parts and the repair itself are quoted and charged separately, after you approve.

How do I know my bike is safe?

It goes only to a Mechanix Pro Certified garage, leaves and returns only with your one-time code, and you hear from us on WhatsApp at each step.

How much does the check cost?

A repair or problem check starts from ₹799, GST included. If you go ahead with the repair, the check fee is adjusted in your final bill. The repair quote comes after the check, and nothing starts until you approve it.

How long will it take?

It depends on the job and on parts. The mechanic gives you an estimate with the quote, and we tell you on WhatsApp if anything changes.
